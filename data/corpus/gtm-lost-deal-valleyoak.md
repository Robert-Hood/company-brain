# Lost deal post-mortem: Valley Oak Dental Group

**Profile:** 22 locations, Arizona. Carestack. Around 6,400 verifications per month.
**Cycle:** First call 2026-04-02, lost 2026-07-16 to an offshore BPO incumbent extending scope.
**Owner:** Rhea, with Jofin on calls two and four.

## What happened

We were the preferred option through call three. Their Director of RCM, Nadia Halabi, told us
directly that our exception handling was the only version of this she had seen that she believed.
We lost on price, and specifically on how we presented price.

## Root cause

Rhea quoted flat $1.75 per verification with a 5,000 per month minimum, from the pricing sheet, on
2026-04-08. Our tiered pricing went live on 2026-04-15, one week later. At 6,400 verifications per
month Valley Oak sits inside the first band so the headline rate would have been the same, but the
tiered structure was the thing that would have won the argument, because Nadia's board question was
"what happens to this number when we acquire and go to 25,000 a month".

Under the flat sheet the answer was "it goes up linearly". Under the tiered model the answer is
"your unit cost drops to $1.40". We gave the first answer. The incumbent gave a volume commitment
discount and won the room.

Nobody had told Rhea the pricing changed. The sheet in Drive still said flat.

## Contributing factors

- Three week gap between call three and the proposal, over which the incumbent got a second meeting
- We never got to their CFO. Nadia carried our case internally and lost it without us in the room
- Carestack integration scoping took eleven days and we did not communicate during it

## Actions

1. Pricing sheet in Drive is stale. It still says flat $1.75 with a 5,000 minimum. Current model
   since 2026-04-15 is tiered: $1.75 up to 20k per month, $1.40 for 20k to 75k, $1.15 above 75k,
   minimum 2,500 per month. Objection handling doc quotes the old number too. Both need updating.
   **Owner: Jofin. Still open as of this writing.**
2. Any deal above 15 locations gets a CFO on the call before proposal. Non-negotiable.
3. Integration scoping gets a status note to the prospect every third day even when there is nothing
   to report.

## The uncomfortable version

We lost a deal because a document was three months out of date and the person using it had no way to
know. That is not a sales problem.
