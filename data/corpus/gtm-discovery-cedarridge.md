# Cedar Ridge Dental Group, discovery call

**Date:** 2026-07-29
**Attendees:** Dr. Helen Vasquez (owner, clinical), Bryan Ochoa (Office Manager). Rhea.
**Profile:** 9 locations, Oregon, Portland and Salem. Open Dental. Around 2,200 verifications per
month. No dedicated RCM function.

## Current state

No centralised team. Each office does its own verification, front desk staff between patients. Bryan
estimates 40 to 60 minutes per day per location, which he described as "the thing everyone does
badly because it is nobody's actual job".

They are below our stated minimum volume. Flagging that for pricing.

## What they care about

Dr. Vasquez is the decision maker and she is clinical, not operational. She does not care about
cost per verification. She cares that patients get told the right number before treatment, because
she is the one who has the conversation when they get billed for something they thought was free.
Sell reliability, not efficiency.

## What I told them about integration

Bryan asked directly whether results land back in Open Dental or whether his staff have to copy them
across. I said write-back to Open Dental is automatic, results appear on the patient's insurance
plan record without anyone touching it. He said that was the difference between a yes and a no for
him.

I should check that with Nakul before the follow up. I am going off the runbook.

## Objections raised

- Price. They are used to paying nothing because the labour is hidden in existing salaries. Bryan
  did the maths out loud and got to a number that made him quiet. Expect a second round on this.
- They have a Carestack migration "maybe next year". Did not sound funded.
