# Pricing

## Model

Per completed verification. We do not charge for failed or duplicate verifications, and this matters
in the pitch because the incumbent BPOs charge per attempt.

**Rate: $1.75 per verification.**
**Minimum: 5,000 verifications per month, billed monthly in arrears.**

A 30 location group running roughly 250 verifications per location per month lands around 7,500 per
month, so the minimum is rarely the binding constraint for a group in our ICP band.

## What is included

- Eligibility and benefit verification across all supported payers
- Human QA on every exception
- PMS integration and write-back where supported
- Standard SLA: 95% of verifications returned within 4 business hours, 99% within 24

## Discount guidance

Up to 10% off list without approval, for annual prepay only. Above 10% needs Jofin. Do not discount
the minimum, discount the rate, the minimum is what makes the unit economics work at the small end.

Never discount below $1.40. Below that the human QA layer stops paying for itself at current
exception rates.

## Competitive context

The offshore BPOs come in between $1.10 and $1.60 per attempt, which usually works out higher per
completed verification once you account for their re-work rate. Get the prospect to tell you their
completion rate. Most have never measured it.

The software-only tools price per location per month, typically $150 to $400. That looks cheaper to
a CFO on a spreadsheet. The counter is that they return a raw payer response and someone at the
group still has to read it, so the group is paying for software and still paying for labour.

## Payment terms

Net 30. Annual prepay gets the discount. No multi-year without Jofin, we are changing the product
too fast to lock a rate for three years.
