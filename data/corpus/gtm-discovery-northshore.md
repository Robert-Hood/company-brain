# Northshore Dental Alliance, discovery call

**Date:** 2026-06-24
**Attendees:** Priyanka Rao (Director of RCM), Dev Chaudhry (Director of IT), Karen Whitfield (CFO,
joined for the last 15 minutes). Rhea, Jofin.
**Profile:** 112 locations, Illinois and Wisconsin. Dentrix Ascend. Roughly 31,000 verifications per
month. Largest opportunity in the pipeline.

## Current state

Hybrid. An offshore BPO in Manila handles the bulk, an in-house team of four in Milwaukee handles
exceptions and anything requiring a phone call. Contract with the BPO renews in January, which is
the real deadline on this deal.

Priyanka has the attempt to completion number and it is 1.6. She was the first prospect who has ever
had that number ready. She knows exactly what her effective cost per completed verification is and
she is not happy about it.

## What matters to them

1. Exception rate transparency. She wants to see, per payer, how often the automated pass fails and
   why. Not a dashboard for its own sake, she wants to renegotiate her staffing model against it.
2. Audit trail. Karen asked twice about how we evidence what was verified and when, in the event of
   a payer audit or a patient dispute.
3. Dentrix Ascend partner status. Dev asked whether we are a certified integration partner. We are
   in process. Do not overstate this.

## Risk

Dev is the blocker. He has been through two failed integrations and he opened by telling us so. He
is not hostile, he is tired. He wants a named engineering contact and a rollback plan, not a sales
process.

## Next steps

- Jofin to send exception rate data by payer, anonymised, as a proof point
- Nakul to join call two as the named engineering contact for Dev
- Confirm Dentrix Ascend partner agreement status before call two
- Target: proposal by end of September, decision before the January BPO renewal
