# PTO and leave

## Annual leave

18 days per calendar year, plus public holidays in your location. Accrues monthly from your start
date. Pro-rated in your first year.

Carry over up to 5 days into the following year. They expire at the end of March. We would rather
you took them.

## Booking

Request in the HR system, tell your manager, and put it in the shared calendar. Two weeks notice for
anything over three days, more for anything over a week. There is no approval theatre, but the
verification queue does not pause, so the ops team coordinates cover between themselves before
booking. Priya keeps the shift roster.

## Public holidays

You take the public holidays where you live. The US team takes US federal holidays. The India team
takes the standard list plus two floating days of their choosing.

## Sick leave

Take it. Tell your manager. There is no cap and there is no doctor's note requirement for anything
under five consecutive days. Do not work while sick, particularly on the verification queue, where
tired mistakes become patient billing problems.

## Parental leave

16 weeks fully paid for the primary caregiver, 6 weeks fully paid for the secondary caregiver,
available from day one of employment. Phased return over four weeks at full pay if you want it.

## Unpaid leave and sabbatical

Case by case with Maya and Jofin. We have said yes to both so far.

## The part that actually matters

The ops team runs a queue with an SLA and the temptation is to not take leave. Priya and Akhilesh
are responsible for making sure that does not happen. If you are on the HITL team and you have taken
fewer than 5 days by the end of June, that is a conversation, not an achievement.
