# Expense policy

Small company, few rules, one principle: spend it like it is yours and be able to explain it.

## Approval thresholds

| Amount | Approval |
|---|---|
| Under $200 | None, submit with receipt |
| $200 to $1,000 | Your manager, before you spend |
| $1,000 to $5,000 | Maya, before you spend |
| Above $5,000 | Jofin, before you spend |

Recurring software counts as annualised value, not monthly. A $90 per month tool is a $1,080
decision.

## Travel

Book economy on flights under six hours. Above six hours, premium economy is fine, business needs
Jofin. Book at least 14 days ahead where the trip is known, we are not paying walk-up fares because
someone did not put it in the calendar.

Hotels: reasonable business standard. No per diem, submit actuals. Meals while travelling are
covered, alcohol is covered when it is a customer dinner and not otherwise.

Customer visits: always book refundable. Dental groups reschedule.

## Home office

$500 one-time on joining for desk setup, plus $50 per month toward internet for fully remote staff.
Monitors and laptops are company assets, ordered by Maya, not expensed.

## What needs no approval but does need a receipt

Coffee with a candidate. Books. Conference tickets under $200. A domain name.

## What is not covered

Personal phone bills, gym memberships, coworking day passes without prior approval, and anything
where the receipt has been lost. If you lose a receipt, tell Maya before submitting rather than
after.

## Submission

Monthly, by the 5th, through the expense tool. Reimbursement lands with the following payroll run.
Anything submitted after the 5th waits a month, which is not a punishment, it is just how the
payroll cycle works.
