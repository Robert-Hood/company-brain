# ICP

## Who we sell to

Multi-location dental groups and DSOs, 8 to 150 locations, US only. Below 8 locations the pain is
real but the budget authority sits with an owner-dentist who is also chairside, and the sales cycle
is long relative to the contract value. Above 150 the group usually has an offshore RCM team already
and the conversation becomes a displacement fight rather than a greenfield one.

Sweet spot is 20 to 60 locations. Centralised RCM function, one or two people running eligibility
for the whole group, visible backlog, and enough volume that per-verification pricing produces a
real number without a procurement process.

## Buyer

Primary: VP of Revenue Cycle, Director of RCM, or in smaller groups the Regional Operations Manager.
They own the backlog and feel it daily.

Economic buyer: COO or CFO. They care about cost per verification and about collections leakage from
bad quotes, not about AI.

Blocker: the IT or systems person who owns the PMS. If they have been burned by an integration
before, they will slow everything down. Bring Tom in early rather than late.

## Qualifying signals

- Runs Open Dental, Dentrix Ascend, Carestack or Eaglesoft. Anything else is a custom build and we
  should not be selling it yet.
- Verification volume above 2,500 per month
- Currently doing eligibility in house or with an offshore BPO
- Has had a collections problem traced back to a bad quote in the last year. This is the single best
  signal. Ask about it directly.

## Disqualifying signals

- Single location or fully owner-operated
- Wants us to do full claims submission today. We do eligibility. Say so.
- Wants a per-seat licence rather than per-verification. The model does not work and the
  conversation is a distraction.

## Why they buy

Not cost. Every group we have won has bought because their existing process is unreliable in a way
that shows up at the front desk. Cost is how they justify it internally. Reliability is why they
call.
